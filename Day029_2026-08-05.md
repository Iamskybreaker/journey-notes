## 🧠 What I Learned

- **Clusters (Allocation Units):**  
  • Units used by a file system to allocate storage space for files  
  • Cluster size determines how much space is allocated at a time  
  • Larger or smaller cluster sizes can affect storage efficiency  
  • Unused space within an allocated cluster can result in slack space  

- **Partitions:**  
  • Logical divisions of a physical storage drive  
  • Used to organize and manage storage on a drive  
  • A physical drive can contain multiple partitions  

- **Partition Table:**  
  • Stores information about the partitions on a drive  
  • **MBR (Master Boot Record)** and **GPT (GUID Partition Table)** are the two major partitioning schemes
