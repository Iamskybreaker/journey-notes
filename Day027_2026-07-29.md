## 🧠 What I Learned

- **Firewall:**  
  • A security system that monitors and filters network traffic based on predefined rules  
  • Helps protect devices and networks from unauthorized access  

- **Firewall Rules:**  
  • Determine which inbound and outbound traffic is allowed or blocked  
  • Form the basis of how a firewall makes security decisions  

- **Types of Firewalls (by Inspection Method):**  
  • Packet Filtering Firewall  
  • Stateful Firewall  
  • Next-Generation Firewall (NGFW)  

- **Types of Firewalls (by Deployment):**  
  • Host-Based Firewall → Protects an individual device  
  • Network-Based Firewall → Protects an entire network
